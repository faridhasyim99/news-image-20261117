Entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-20261117**: sum up 17 November's
world news in one image.

## What it is

`entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG) is one editorial front page: a header dated
**TUESDAY 17 NOVEMBER 2026 — THE WORLD TODAY** over six labelled vignettes,
a numbered world-map strip, and 21 brief tiles — **27 stories across 6
continents** (Europe, Asia, North America, South America, Africa, Oceania).
Every story happened, or was first reported, on Tuesday 17 November 2026,
and every one was verified against a wire or primary source before it was
drawn. Allegations and official claims are labelled as reported, alleged or
contested — never stated as fact. The illustration art was generated without
text; every label was overlaid afterwards, so all words are spelled exactly
as shown.

## The six vignettes

1. **Kerch Bridge span collapses** — a Ukrainian strike collapsed the road
   arch and span into the Kerch Strait, the second straight day of attacks
   on the bridge; Crimea governor Aksyonov said 2 were killed; rail resumed
   on one track; Russia struck Kyiv's bridges the same day (Reuters,
   17 Nov 2026).
2. **Drones over Bahrain radar** — the IRGC claims combat drones hit a US
   radar at Sheikh Isa Air Base; Bahrain's Defence Force and US CENTCOM say
   they were intercepted, with no damage or casualties; the US struck a
   missile launch site on Qeshm Island (Reuters, 17 Nov 2026).
3. **Gaza aid held at the border** — the UK sanctioned 8 construction and
   property firms plus the Tzav 9 aid-blocking group over West Bank
   settlements (gov.uk, 17 Nov 2026); Gaza's first winter storm flooded
   shelters while 150,000 tents and supplies were held at the border (joint
   statement by the foreign ministers of Australia, Canada, Italy and the
   UK, 16 Nov, widely reported 17 Nov 2026).
4. **Jets scramble over Japan** — two suspected Russian aircraft entered
   Japanese airspace north-west of Okinoshima, Fukuoka, for about 3 minutes
   each at 04:07 and 04:09 local time; Japan scrambled fighters and lodged
   a "very stern protest"; the first confirmed violation since 23 September
   2024 (Kyodo / Reuters, 17 Nov 2026).
5. **Quad meets in Manila** — Trump met the Quad leaders (US, Japan,
   Australia, India) in Manila, the first Quad meeting in two years; an
   APEC meeting in Shenzhen and a trilateral with Xi and Putin follow on
   19 Nov (Reuters, 17 Nov 2026).
6. **Depardieu drops appeal** — Gérard Depardieu dropped his appeal and
   accepted the 18-month suspended sentence, apologising ("I hear it and I
   accept this conviction"); he was convicted in May 2025 of groping a set
   decorator and an assistant on the 2021 set of *Les Volets Verts*
   (Guardian / Just Jared, reported 17 Nov 2026).

## The 21 briefs

1. **REPORTED: Iran "dirty bomb" plans** — CNN (16 Nov), citing seven US
   officials: new intelligence indicates Iran is developing plans to target
   senior Trump administration officials in Washington with a dirty bomb or
   chemical weapon; described as preliminary, with no imminent attack.
   Trump responded in Manila on 17 Nov, warning Iran would "pay a price";
   Iran called the report fabricated; the White House said there are no
   credible threats to the homeland (CNN / USA Today, 17 Nov 2026).
   Labelled REPORTED, never stated as fact.
2. **Deutsche Bank leak: $582M Epstein** — more than 15 million leaked
   internal files show the bank flagged about $582 million in suspicious
   transactions linked to Jeffrey Epstein — more than double the amount
   previously known — pursued Russian oligarch business until the 2022
   invasion, and accidentally shared Donald Trump's tax records in 2017;
   shares fell 6% (Reuters, 17 Nov 2026).
3. **Epstein death: forensic claim** — forensic medicine professor Michael
   Freeman presented findings in London on 16 Nov, saying Epstein's
   injuries are "most consistent with" homicidal strangulation from behind;
   the New York City medical examiner's office stands by its 2019 suicide
   ruling; the analysis has not been peer-reviewed (CBS / LADbible,
   16–17 Nov 2026). Labelled as a contested claim.
4. **Russia claims 1,441 drones downed** — Russia's Defence Ministry
   claims a record 1,441 Ukrainian drones intercepted overnight into
   17 Nov across 22 regions; Ukrainian drones also hit the Gazprom
   Neftekhim Salavat petrochemical complex; the figures are not
   independently verified (Bloomberg / United24, 17 Nov 2026).
5. **Woman, 72, killed near Jericho** — Israeli settlers shot dead
   72-year-old Fadha Wadha in al-Duyuk al-Tahta, near Jericho, overnight;
   10 people were wounded; settlers had taken over eight homes in the
   village on 4 Nov (Reuters, 17 Nov 2026).
6. **Third US carrier reaches Mideast** — USS Theodore Roosevelt entered
   CENTCOM's area on 16 Nov, joining USS George H.W. Bush and USS George
   Washington, the first three-carrier concentration since April; the
   tanker ON PHOENIX was hit by a projectile 7 nautical miles off Khasab,
   Oman, with an engine-room fire and the crew safe (AP / UKMTO,
   17 Nov 2026).
7. **EU states plan Rwanda return hub** — Germany, Austria, Greece,
   Denmark and the Netherlands announced a pilot return hub in Rwanda on
   16 Nov in Vienna: about 100 people, roughly 20 per country, first
   transfers in 2027; Amnesty International and Save the Children condemned
   it on 17 Nov (Reuters, 17 Nov 2026).
8. **Gaza storm floods shelters** — the first winter storm flooded
   shelters in Gaza; 150,000 tents and supplies are held at the border
   (joint ministerial statement, reported 17 Nov 2026).
9. **Canada–China: tariff relief** — Carney met Xi in Shenzhen ahead of
   APEC; China gives tariff relief worth about C$400 million on Canadian
   pork and seafood; Canada extends steel and aluminium remission measures
   for two years (Reuters, 17 Nov 2026).
10. **Houthis advance on Aden** — government forces withdrew from Bab
    el-Mandeb and Dhubab to defend Aden; Houthi forces advanced to roughly
    60–100 km of the city; Saudi Arabia replaced coalition commander
    Lt Gen Fahd al-Salman with Gen Fayyad al-Ruwaili on 17 Nov (Reuters,
    17 Nov 2026).
11. **US closes AI-chip loophole** — the Bureau of Industry and Security's
    interim final rule (issued 16 Nov, reported 17 Nov) requires licences
    for remote access to advanced AI chips and names five diversion hubs
    (Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Brazil); it rescinds the AI
    Diffusion Rule (BIS / Reuters, 17 Nov 2026).
12. **Berlin talks set for Thursday** — Witkoff and Kushner are to meet
    Ukrainian and European officials in Berlin on 19 Nov on security
    guarantees and postwar recovery (Axios, 17 Nov 2026).
13. **Eritrea beats trachoma** — the WHO validated Eritrea's elimination
    of trachoma as a public health problem on 16 Nov: the 11th country in
    WHO's African Region and the 34th globally (WHO, announced 16 Nov,
    reported 17 Nov 2026).
14. **Killer-robot talks stall** — 128 states opened the CCW Seventh
    Review Conference in Geneva on 16 Nov; Russia's procedural objection
    stalled the opening session; the US, Russia and Israel oppose binding
    rules; a decision is expected by 20 Nov (Reuters, 16–17 Nov 2026).
15. **Data centres = Denmark's CO2** — a Harvard study in *Nature
    Sustainability* (16 Nov): 403 US hyperscale data centres used about
    82 TWh from May 2024 to April 2025, about 25.7 million tonnes of CO2 a
    year — as much as Denmark — with 54% of the power from fossil fuels
    (Harvard / AP, 17 Nov 2026).
16. **World Cup: Germany, Dutch in** — final European qualifying
    matchday: Germany 6–0 Slovakia, Netherlands 4–0 Lithuania and Croatia
    3–2 Montenegro book World Cup 2026; Norway 4–1 Italy end a 28-year
    wait (17 Nov 2026).
17. **Santner out of India Tests** — New Zealand Cricket, 17 Nov: Mitchell
    Santner fractured his right hand and is out of the two-Test series
    against India; Dean Foxcroft was called up (NZC, 17 Nov 2026).
18. **Brazil probes Vance-linked cash** — the Washington Post reports a
    Brazilian federal police investigation into a JD Vance-connected donor
    network (Rockbridge) over possible interference in Brazil's October
    election (Washington Post via CEPR, 17 Nov 2026).
19. **Dow −849; Nvidia beats after bell** — the Dow fell 848.75 points
    (−1.64%) to 50,759.42; the S&P 500 fell 1.03% to 7,340.53, its fourth
    straight loss and lowest close since July; the Nasdaq fell 0.74%;
    after the close Nvidia reported Q3 revenue of $115.84 billion, up 103%
    year on year, EPS $2.68, and guided Q4 revenue to about $130 billion;
    shares rose about 4% after hours (Reuters / CNBC, 17 Nov 2026).
20. **Spider-Man back 4 December** — Sony and Marvel announced an
    extended cut of *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* returning to theatres on
    4 December, tickets on sale 19 November, with about 10 minutes of extra

21. **Catholics 80% to 55% in LatAm** — Latinobarómetro: Catholic
    identification in Latin America fell from 80% in 1995 to 55% in
    2025/26; evangelicals are now the largest group in Honduras, El Salvador
    and Guatemala (Christian Post, 17 Nov 2026).

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). No network, no
randomness: both runs write byte-identical output.

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

See `TESTS.md` for the determinism and gate checks that were run.
